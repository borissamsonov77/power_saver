The EcoFlow DELTA 2 is one of the most popular portable power stations in Ukraine and Europe. Let's see if it justifies its price.

## Key Specifications

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Battery capacity | 1,024 Wh |
| Maximum output power | 1,800W (2,700W peak) |
| Battery technology | LiFePO4 |
| Cycle lifespan | 3,000+ cycles |
| AC charge speed | 0→100% in 80 minutes (X-Stream) |
| Solar input support | Up to 500W |
| Weight | 26.4 lbs (12 kg) |
| Warranty | 5 years |

## What Can You Power?

With 1,024 Wh capacity and ~90% LiFePO4 efficiency:

- **60W laptop:** ~15 hours
- **100W TV:** ~9 hours
- **150W compressor fridge:** ~5-6 hours of active cooling
- **20W LED lighting:** 45+ hours
- **10W Wi-Fi router:** 80+ hours
- **Combined (laptop + router + lights):** ~8-10 hours

## Pros

✅ **Fast charging** — 0 to 100% in 1.3 hours via X-Stream. Even a brief window of grid power is enough to fully recharge.

✅ **LiFePO4 = longevity** — 3,000+ cycles means 8-10 years of regular use before significant capacity degradation.

✅ **Expandable capacity** — DELTA 2 can be expanded to 2,048 or 3,072 Wh with DELTA 2 Extra Battery modules.

✅ **Quiet operation** — 30 dB at medium load. You can sleep near it.

✅ **EcoFlow app** — Bluetooth/Wi-Fi monitoring and control. Convenient for checking remaining charge remotely.

## Cons

❌ **Price** — $999 MSRP (varies by region). A significant investment.

❌ **12 kg weight** — not light. Frequent room-to-room movement can be inconvenient.

❌ **Fan noise at high load** — when running appliances above 500W, the fan becomes noticeable.

❌ **No seamless pass-through switching** — when grid power cuts, there's a brief switchover gap (not UPS-grade).

## Who Is It For?

**Recommended if:**
- You live in a city with 4-12 hours of daily outages
- You need to power a laptop, router, and lighting
- Future capacity expansion is important to you
- You plan to add solar panels

**Not recommended if:**
- You need to run a fridge or water heater for extended periods — larger capacity needed
- Budget is tight — more affordable alternatives exist

## Rating: 8.5/10

The EcoFlow DELTA 2 is a reliable choice for most urban households in Ukraine and Europe. Its LiFePO4 chemistry, fast charging, and expandability justify the premium price.
