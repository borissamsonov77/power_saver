The Bluetti AC180 is frequently called the "best value" option in mid-range power stations. Here's why — and where it falls short.

## Key Specifications

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Battery capacity | 1,152 Wh |
| Maximum output power | 1,800W (2,700W peak) |
| Battery technology | LiFePO4 |
| Cycle lifespan | 2,500+ cycles |
| AC charge speed | 0→80% in 45 minutes (Turbo mode) |
| Solar input support | Up to 500W |
| Weight | 36.4 lbs (16.5 kg) |
| Warranty | 5 years |

## What Can You Power?

With 1,152 Wh at ~88% efficiency:

- **60W laptop:** ~17 hours
- **100W TV:** ~10 hours
- **20W LED lighting:** ~50 hours
- **10W Wi-Fi router:** ~90 hours
- **150W fridge:** ~6 hours
- **Combined (laptop + router + 3 LEDs):** ~10-12 hours

## Pros

✅ **Best price per Wh** — at $799 for 1,152 Wh, that's ~$0.69/Wh vs EcoFlow DELTA 2's ~$0.97/Wh. A meaningful difference.

✅ **LiFePO4 + 2,500 cycles** — long service life. At daily use, expect 7+ years.

✅ **Fast charging** — 45 minutes to 80% in Turbo mode. Very convenient during short grid power windows.

✅ **Power Lifting feature** — boosts peak power to 1,320W for starting compressor fridges and pumps that would otherwise exceed rated output.

✅ **Clean display** — readable and straightforward. Shows input, output, remaining charge and time. No unnecessary complexity.

## Cons

❌ **Heavier** — 16.5 kg vs 12 kg for the DELTA 2. At equal output wattage, Bluetti is heavier.

❌ **No official expansion** — unlike the DELTA 2, AC180 cannot officially be expanded with extra battery modules.

❌ **Lower DC solar efficiency** — MPPT controller is less efficient during variable cloud cover compared to EcoFlow.

❌ **Older connector design** — some early units had reported contact issues with USB-A ports.

## Who Is It For?

**Recommended if:**
- Budget per Wh efficiency matters most to you
- Short but frequent power windows — fast recharging is critical
- You need to run a compressor fridge (Power Lifting)

**Consider EcoFlow DELTA 2 if:**
- Expandable capacity is important for the future
- Lower weight matters (frequent moving between rooms)
- Premium features justify higher price

## Rating: 8.2/10

The Bluetti AC180 is the best value-for-money choice in its class. More capacity, lower price, LiFePO4 chemistry, and fast charging. Drawbacks: heavier weight and no expansion option.
