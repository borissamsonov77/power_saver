A portable power station is a battery with a built-in inverter, USB ports, and AC outlets. It requires no fuel, runs silently, and can be recharged from solar panels. Here's how to choose the right one.

## Key Parameters

### 1. Capacity (Watt-hours / Wh)

Capacity tells you how much energy the station can store. More capacity = longer runtime.

**Rough calculation:**
- Laptop (45W) × 8 hours = 360 Wh
- Router (10W) × 8 hours = 80 Wh
- LED lighting (20W) × 6 hours = 120 Wh
- **Total: ~560 Wh** — minimum for a "digital office"

**Practical tip:** Real-world capacity is typically 10-20% less than rated due to inverter efficiency losses. Add 20% to your calculations.

### 2. Output Power (Watts / W)

Power determines which devices you can run simultaneously.

| Device | Typical Wattage |
|--------|----------------|
| Laptop | 45–90W |
| Phone charging | 15–65W |
| Wi-Fi router | 5–15W |
| LED light | 5–15W |
| 12V mini fridge | 40–60W |
| Coffee maker | 600–1200W |
| Hair dryer | 1200–2200W |

**Important:** Some devices have surge (startup) current 2-3× higher than running wattage (compressor fridges, drills). Always check the surge/peak watts rating.

### 3. Battery Chemistry

**LiFePO4 (Lithium Iron Phosphate)** — recommended for regular use:
- 2,000–3,500 cycles (10 years of regular use)
- Safer, much less prone to thermal runaway
- Slightly heavier than NMC at equal capacity

**NMC (Nickel Manganese Cobalt):**
- 500–1,000 cycles (3-5 years)
- Lighter and more compact
- Good for occasional use

### 4. Ports and Connectors

Check that the unit has:
- ✅ AC outlets (at least 2)
- ✅ USB-A and USB-C (preferably USB-C PD 100W+)
- ✅ DC 12V for car/automotive devices
- ✅ Solar input (MPPT controller built in)

## Recommended Ranges by Budget

### Under $400 (400–500 Wh)
Good for: phone charging, router, lighting, small laptop.
Look at: EcoFlow RIVER 2, Jackery Explorer 500, Bluetti EB3A.

### $400–$800 (500–1000 Wh)
Good for: laptop + router + lighting for 8-12 hours.
Look at: EcoFlow RIVER 2 Pro (768 Wh), Bluetti AC180 (1152 Wh).

### $800–$2000 (1000–2000 Wh)
Good for: fridge + full apartment for 8-16 hours.
Look at: EcoFlow DELTA 2 Max, Bluetti AC200P.

### $2000+ (2000+ Wh)
Good for: full home autonomy 24+ hours, expandable with solar panels.
Look at: EcoFlow DELTA Pro (3600 Wh), Bluetti AC300+B300.

## What Doesn't Work for Home Backup

- **Gasoline generators** — noisy, require ventilation, need regular maintenance
- **Car inverters** — drain your car battery, unstable voltage
- **Standard powerbanks** — no AC outlets, can't power a laptop or fridge

## Selection Checklist

- [ ] Calculated required capacity for your devices
- [ ] Checked the wattage of the highest-draw appliance
- [ ] Chose LiFePO4 if planning regular use
- [ ] Confirmed solar panel support (if daytime recharging is needed)
- [ ] Compared price per Wh of capacity across models
