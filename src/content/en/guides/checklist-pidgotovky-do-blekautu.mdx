This checklist is designed for urban apartment and house residents. We recommend reviewing and updating it every 6 months.

## 🔦 Lighting & Power

- [ ] Rechargeable headlamp (minimum 2 per adult)
- [ ] 10,000+ mAh powerbank (always charged to 80%+)
- [ ] Portable power station 500+ Wh (if budget allows)
- [ ] Charging cables for all devices in one place
- [ ] Spare batteries for flashlights and remotes

## 💧 Water

- [ ] Drinking water reserve: **3 liters/day per person** (minimum 3 days)
- [ ] Containers to fill from tap (pumping stations may fail in wide outages)
- [ ] Water purification tablets (in case tap water becomes unsafe)

> **Tip:** 1.5L plastic water bottles are a cheap and reliable reserve. Store in a cool, dark place and replace every 6-12 months.

## 🍲 Food

- [ ] 3-day supply of canned goods and shelf-stable foods (no refrigeration needed)
- [ ] Manual can opener
- [ ] Camping gas stove or alcohol burner (if you have an electric cooktop)
- [ ] Spare gas canisters (store safely — outdoors or in ventilated area)

<SafetyWarning message="⚠️ Never use gas stoves, barbecue grills, or camping burners indoors. Use only outdoors or in well-ventilated areas." />

## 📱 Communication & Information

- [ ] List of important phone numbers on paper (family, doctor, emergency services)
- [ ] Utility provider's outage channel saved in Telegram/social media
- [ ] Battery-powered or hand-crank radio
- [ ] Powerbank charged and easily accessible

## 🏥 Medical

- [ ] Basic first aid kit (bandages, antiseptic, pain relief, fever medication)
- [ ] 30-day supply of prescription medications (if applicable)
- [ ] Glucose meter and test strips (for diabetes) — and insulin storage plan
- [ ] Family doctor's contact saved

## 🔥 Warmth (Winter)

- [ ] Warm blankets for each family member
- [ ] Thermal sleepwear (if your apartment can get cold quickly)
- [ ] Gloves, hats — accessible immediately, not buried in storage
- [ ] Know the nearest warming center location

## 🏠 Safety & Documents

- [ ] Digital copies of documents in the cloud (passport, certificates)
- [ ] Cash in small bills (card terminals may be offline)
- [ ] Know how to shut off water, gas, and electricity in your apartment

## 🌞 Extended Kit (if budget allows)

- [ ] Power station 1000+ Wh
- [ ] 100-200W solar panel
- [ ] UPS for router (1-2 hours autonomy)
- [ ] 12V powerbank for router
- [ ] Laptop power bank

---

**Remember:** The best preparation is gradual. Buy one or two items per month, and within a year you'll have a comprehensive autonomy kit.
